About NYC Soundscape

This project maps one full day of city sounds through the lens of different New Yorkers. The personas are fictional, but data-driven, representing typical New Yorkers. Each hour ties a place to the sounds that shape it, then lets you hear a small slice of that moment.

The radial clock shows how the city changes over 24 hours. The timeline and map let you feel the rhythm of each neighborhood.

  • Data source: SONYC Urban Sound Tagging dataset
  • Methods used: Machine learning for sound classification, spatial analysis for neighborhood mapping
  • GitHub repo
  • Built by Cedric Lam :D
