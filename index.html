What you’re looking at

This is a sound map of a New York City day. Pick a persona, then move hour by hour to see where they are, what they hear, and which sound dominates that moment.

The clock shows the day as a 24-hour ring. The map follows the person through the city. The lower panel shows the audio layer and the stories behind each hour.

Click a persona, scrub the timeline, or tap a story line to jump back to that moment. Use the toggle in the header to switch themes. Use the pause button if you want to stop the sound.
